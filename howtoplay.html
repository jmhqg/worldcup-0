How to Play

WorldCup-0 - Build your ultimate World Cup squad

  1. 6 spins give you a random nation + World Cup year
  2. Pick any player from that squad to fill a position
  3. Positions: GK · DEF · CM · WIN · STR · Wildcard
  4. Higher ratings = better squad result
  5. 1 free respin each for country + year per game
  6. Your squad is simulated — can you win the World Cup?
